Learn Wine

Acidity

The fresh, mouthwatering quality that makes a wine feel crisp and lively.

Tannins

The drying texture most noticeable in red wines. Think black tea.

Body

How light or full a wine feels in the mouth, from delicate to rich.

Dry vs. Sweet

Dry wines have little perceptible sugar; fruity aromas do not necessarily mean sweet.

Quick Presentation

Start with grape and region, then describe texture, key flavors, and a food pairing.

Wine Service

Present

Confirm the producer, wine, and vintage with the guest before opening.

Open

Open carefully, keep the label visible when practical, and wipe the bottle lip.

Taste & Pour

Offer a small taste to the host when appropriate; serve guests without overfilling glasses.

Serving Temperature

Sparkling and light whites: chilled. Fuller whites: cool. Light reds: slightly cool. Fuller reds: cool room temperature.

Pairing Basics

Match freshness with rich food, body with intensity, and sweetness with spicy dishes.
